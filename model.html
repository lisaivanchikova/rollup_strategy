4 · Compound · No burnout, 11 deals across 5 years — retained cash funds next equity

Retained portfolio cash flow funds the next deal's equity → loop catches for 5 years

Deal count is not a slider — it is derived. Each acquisition is financed standalone (Senior debt + 12% seller note + fund equity; see §6). As portcos mature, their retained free cash flow (EBITDA − debt service − central OPEX) recycles into deployable equity, funding the next deal without new LP capital. Loop runs until equity — fund plus recycled cash — is insufficient for the next purchase. That year = burnout. Op cap = HoldCo integration bandwidth (deals per year), scenario-scaled: Bear 1/1/2/2/2 = 8 max; Base 1/2/4/4/4 = 15 max; Bull 1/3/6/6/6 = 22 max.

6 · Capital structure · Senior debt 2.75× + 12% seller note + equity — source-agnostic, $151K/deal fixed cost

Inputs on this page are sourced individually; the capital structure is market convention rather than conference data. sources (SBA SOP 50 10 8, GF Data, Carta, BizBuySell, IBBA — see §9). Three layers replace the prior "equity + 4× unitranche" simplification: (A) the fixed transaction-cost stack that is the real basis of the small-target thesis, (B) the achievable per-deal financing structure, and (C) the corporate cap table across funding rounds.

A · Fixed deal costs — not overpayment — are why incumbents can't go small

Buyer-side cost is ~$151K per deal and largely fixed (QoE $20K + legal $18K + escrow $3K + in-house team $110K; broker excluded — Double-Lehman is seller-paid).24 Fixed $/deal is trivial on a $3M-EBITDA platform but confiscatory on a $0.25M-EBITDA micro-firm — that discontinuity, not skill, is the moat. Our edge is driving the fixed number down via an AI-native deal desk, shifting the viable floor left.

Target EBITDAPurchase priceEquity checkFixed costFixed as % of equityIncumbent reach
Fixed $151K = 6% of a $2.6M deal's equity but ~40%+ on a sub-$0.3M-EBITDA micro-deal. Incumbents' $250K+ internal cost/deal makes micro structurally unreachable — the white space we target.

B · Per-deal financing — Base representative acquisition

Sub-$1M EBITDA targets do not qualify for unitranche (floor ≈ $10–15M EBITDA26). The achievable stack is an senior note (≤2.75× EBITDA, capped $5M, ~10.5%)24 + a 12% seller note (~7%, on standby)27 + fund equity. 4× unitranche only unlocks once the consolidated platform crosses ~$3M EBITDA.

8 · Downside · Bear returns no equity — debt exceeds exit value / supplier hikes hit Y2

Pre-mortem — three risk vectors

Market risk (not ours)
  • PE bidder demand pushes sub-$3M tier out of reach — arbitrage window closes1
  • Sellers refuse compressed multiples — deal flow stalls, op cap unreachable
  • Exit falls a band — 9.0× to 7.5×3 — the portfolio drops a maturity band and multiple expansion evaporates
Execution risk (risky)
  • Uplift under-delivers: cost/rev/price levers miss by 50%+ across cohorts
  • Managed GM stalls below the 51.2% leading cohort8
  • Post-close customer flight in first cohorts → playbook never validated → later cohorts inherit no template
Tech + supplier risk
  • AI stack fails → HoldCo scales to 40 FTE → central OPEX doubles → MOIC halves
  • Kaseya/ConnectWise vendor consolidation → 15–20% price hikes14 → cross-portfolio GM compression 2–4 pp
  • Mitigant: multi-vendor stack policy + Huntress/NinjaOne alternate rails1517
